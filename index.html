Common questions

What users need to know.

Do I need a Personal Trainer?

Yes. Downloading Personal is the first step; a supported package from your Trainer is needed to use it as intended. The Trainer-supported CMA workflow requires a qualified Personal Trainer using Windows Trainer to supply your programme and provide ongoing guidance and review. Personal users can also create or edit their own entries; those changes are not automatically Trainer-approved. Contact your Trainer before getting started.

Does CMA Fitness Coach Suite require an internet connection?

Normal day-to-day recording works offline. Internet access is needed for Store downloads and updates, online food searches and other online services you choose to use.

Where is application data stored?

Personal records are stored locally on your Windows or Android device. Trainer client records are stored locally on the Windows PC. Supported exports and transfers happen when you choose them.

Is it a replacement for medical or dietary advice?

No. The application supports fitness planning and record keeping. It does not provide medical diagnosis or replace qualified professional advice.

Can Personal Edition information be used by a trainer?

The Personal Edition includes an export workflow intended for compatible import into the Trainer Edition.

Your fitness partnership

Work with a qualified Personal Trainer.

CMA Personal is designed to work with a qualified Personal Trainer who uses CMA Trainer. Arrange this support before relying on the app for your training programme.

Start with guidance

Your Trainer discusses your goals and circumstances, prepares a suitable programme and supplies it through a supported CMA package.

Record and share

Use Windows or Android Personal to follow your programme, record activity and share supported progress updates with your Trainer when you choose.

Keep reviewing

Your Trainer provides exercise guidance, reviews progress and adjusts your programme. The app supports this relationship; it does not replace professional judgement.

A Trainer qualification does not itself confer medical or specialist dietary expertise. Seek the appropriate qualified professional for those needs. CMA software support covers technical issues; arrange coaching directly with your Trainer.
