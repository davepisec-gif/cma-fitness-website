Common questions

Useful starting points.

Does the application require an internet connection?

No. Normal day-to-day use is offline. Internet access may be needed for Microsoft Store installation or updates.

Where is my data stored?

Application data is stored locally on the Windows device unless you deliberately export or back it up elsewhere.

Does Personal create training or nutrition plans?

No. The intended workflow is for a participating trainer to prepare and supply training programmes and nutritional data.

How is Trainer Edition obtained?

Trainer Edition is available after consultation with CMA Fitness Solutions.
